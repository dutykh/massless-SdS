Nariai raw-spectrum and independent admissibility audit (R2 point 5)

Finding
The uploaded QNM_Nariai.jl and the positive-A branch of the point-1
revision select stable roots by distance to a precomputed analytic ladder.
That procedure checks agreement with a reference list but is circular as a
test that the regularized finite pencil enforces physical radiation.
The new cumulative QNM_Nariai_referee2_point5.jl removes this selection.

Independent physical test
Let q=(1+tanh chi_*)/2 and a,b=1/2 +/- sqrt(1/4-A), c=1-i varpi.
The unit-amplitude left Jost solution is
 J_-=exp(-i varpi chi_*) 2F1(a,b;c;q), J_+(chi_*)=J_-(-chi_*).
At chi_*=0 define F=2F1(a,b;c;1/2), P=-i varpi F+F_q/2.
Then the physical, unregularized Jost Wronskian is W=-2FP and
 D=W/(|F|^2+|P|^2).
The denominator is the product of the two solution-vector norms at the
midpoint. It does not multiply W by a frequency-dependent zero. The
hypergeometric series is evaluated at 80 and 120 decimal digits. Acceptance
requires |D|<=1e-8 and agreement of the two evaluations. Julia checks the
stable average AND every constituent root at the three resolutions.
Analytic frequencies are constructed only after this physical selection,
solely for a subsequent frequency-accuracy comparison.

The independent connection formula gives
 W=-2 Gamma(1-i varpi)^2 /
   [Gamma(1/2-i varpi+sqrt(1/4-A)) Gamma(1/2-i varpi-sqrt(1/4-A))].
This follows from 2i varpi C_in using Gamma(1-i varpi)=(-i varpi)Gamma(-i varpi).
For A=0, cancel the entire gamma quotient first: C_in=1 and W=2i varpi.
Thus every nonzero apparent free-wave ladder root is excluded, independent
of its proximity to any expected spectrum. The static zero is a separate
threshold, never counted as a nonzero scattering QNM. For nonzero A,
c=0,-1,... are normalization poles of this Jost representation and are
flagged for separate Laurent analysis, never accepted by a regularized zero.
The tests do not identify all possible exceptional-frequency behavior.

Raw finite-pencil calculation
N denotes N nodes and polynomial degree N-1, matching the uploaded Maple
roots-grid construction. The operators preserve P_(N-1). In a monomial
basis their exact action is
 L0 y^k=k(k-1)y^(k-2)-[k(k+1)+A]y^k,
 L1 y^k=(2k+1)y^k, L2 y^k=y^k.
The full 2N x 2N companion matrix is assembled from these exact integer
coefficients and diagonalized by mpmath.eig at 60 decimal digits. It is
algebraically equivalent to the collocation pencil: evaluation at N distinct
nodes gives an invertible Vandermonde left factor and changing from monomials
to Chebyshev polynomials gives an invertible right factor. No analytic root
list, characteristic-polynomial factorization, or selected 2x2 block solution
is used. All 2N raw roots are retained with algebraic multiplicity.

A fresh binary64 Chebyshev collocation implementation is also preserved.
It has the same counts in the rectangle. At A=2 its maximum |D| increases
from 6.40e-10 (N16) through 8.70e-9 (N24) to 1.18e-6 (N32); 4/8 N32
roots fail the stated 1e-8 numerical tolerance. They are numerical failures,
not proven absent resonances. The exact-coefficient 60-digit run demonstrates
why an increased degree alone does not guarantee improved finite-precision
frequencies. This binary64 experiment is not the historical 50-digit
Maple export/70-digit Julia workflow.
